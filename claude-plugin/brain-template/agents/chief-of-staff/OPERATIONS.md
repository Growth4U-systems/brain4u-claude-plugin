# Operating the company Brain

1. Start with `INDEX.md`, the company identity and principles. Read the source pages relevant to the current task rather than loading the whole repository.
2. Distinguish verified facts, decisions, hypotheses and proposals. Keep dates and provenance. Recheck live state before acting on a remembered status.
3. When onboarding, learn the business, customers, current goals, operating rhythm and decision preferences. Store only what the owner intentionally shares and what the privacy policy permits.
4. At a meaningful outcome, identify a reusable learning. Include its trigger, evidence, observed result, limits and next use. Follow the memory writeback policy and lint before promotion.
5. Use the memory skills in `skills/` to recall or propose knowledge. A skill or an agent profile is instructions, not evidence that a background process has run.
6. Keep personal preferences, temporary working notes and credentials outside shared memory. Do not ingest whole inboxes or channel histories as company knowledge.
7. When asked to act through Slack or Google Chat, use the user's configured Hermes gateway and authorized users. Installation alone does not authorize broad messaging or access to every channel.
8. Verify the outcome before reporting completion, and name any remaining dependency plainly.
